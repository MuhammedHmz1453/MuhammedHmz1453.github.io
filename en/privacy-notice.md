## 4. Legal grounds and method of collection

Your personal data is collected **electronically** through the contact form and email, and **automatically** through the hosting provider's server logs when you visit the Site.

Your data is processed on the following legal grounds listed in Article 5 of the KVKK:

- **Establishment or performance of a contract (Art. 5/2-c):** data necessary to conduct discussions about your quote request and your project,
- **Legitimate interest (Art. 5/2-f):** data necessary to reply to your messages and keep the Site secure, provided that it does not harm your fundamental rights and freedoms,
- **Legal obligation (Art. 5/2-ç):** where required by law,
- **Explicit consent (Art. 9):** the explicit consent you give with the checkbox on the form for the transfer abroad of the data you send through the contact form.

## 6. Retention period

- **Contact messages and correspondence:** kept for at most **2 years** after your request is concluded, then deleted. If we work on a project together, they may be kept for the duration of that relationship and for the statutory limitation periods.
- **Server logs:** kept for the short periods set in the hosting provider's own policy and deleted automatically.

Data whose retention period has expired or whose purpose of processing no longer exists is deleted or anonymized.

## 7. Your rights under Article 11 of the KVKK

By applying to the data controller, you have the right to:

1. Learn whether your personal data is processed,
2. Request information if it has been processed,
3. Learn the purpose of processing and whether it is used in line with that purpose,
4. Know the third parties in Türkiye or abroad to whom it is transferred,
5. Request correction if it is incomplete or inaccurate,
6. Request its deletion or destruction under the conditions set out in Article 7 of the KVKK,
7. Request that the actions taken under items 5 and 6 be notified to the third parties to whom the data was transferred,
8. Object to a result against you arising from the analysis of the processed data exclusively by automated systems,
9. Claim compensation for damages if you suffer harm due to unlawful processing.

You may withdraw your explicit consent at any time; this does not affect processing carried out before the withdrawal.

## 8. How to apply

You can send requests regarding your rights, in line with the Communiqué on the Procedures and Principles of Application to the Data Controller:

- From the registered email address you previously used to write to me, to <a class="legal__mail" href="mailto:{{ eposta }}" data-copy-email title="Click to copy">{{ eposta }}</a>,
- Or to the same address using a registered electronic mail (KEP) address, a secure electronic signature or a mobile signature.

Your application should include your first and last name, your Turkish ID number if you are a Turkish citizen (or your nationality and passport number if you are not), your notification address or email address, and the subject of your request.

Your application will be concluded free of charge as soon as possible and within **30 days** at the latest, depending on the nature of the request. If the process requires an additional cost, the fee in the tariff set by the Personal Data Protection Board may be charged. If your application is rejected, you find the answer insufficient or no answer is given in time, you may file a complaint with the Personal Data Protection Board under Article 14 of the KVKK.
